Under the hood

The technical detail

A precis of how we actually work — the standards, stack and checks behind ai automation.

LLM applications and agents

We build model-agnostic systems: the app talks to an API or self-hosted model through one interface, so you can switch vendors or version models without a rewrite. Simple tasks route to cheap models; hard ones escalate.

  • Agnostic stack: typical components are embeddings + vector search (RAG), rerankers, tool/function-calling and streaming UIs
  • Agents follow a planning loop (ReAct-style) with explicit steps, tool access and a stop condition — never open-ended
  • Latency budgets per task: chat first-token around or under a second, voice pipelines tested on real calls with stricter budgets

Guardrails, safety and data

AI mistakes are engineered against, not hoped away. Outbound actions pass approval gates, output is constrained by schema, and high-stakes or priced steps require a human.

  • Prompt-injection resistant flows, PII redaction and scoped retrieval so sensitive data never reaches a model by default
  • An evaluation harness with golden cases and graders runs before go-live and on every model change
  • Audit logs per agent action so you can always see and explain what happened

Integration and ROI

Automation that floats is worthless; we wire agents into your CRM, calendar, telephony, email and internal tools via webhooks and connectors. The ROI assessment happens first and KPIs are agreed before build.

  • Token and infra cost per completed task tracked against the value it creates
  • Model upgrade path: when a better or cheaper model ships, we benchmark it against your cases
  • Handover includes docs, costs dashboard and thresholds for scaling the workflow

FAQ

Common questions

Is my data safe with AI?+

Yes. We architect with privacy and guardrails in mind, keeping sensitive data controlled and using retrieval rather than exposing everything to a model.

Will AI actually save us money?+

Only if it targets the right workflow. We start with an ROI assessment and build where the payback is clear.

Which AI model and provider do you use?+

Task-dependent. We compare frontier APIs against open-weight models on quality, latency and price — and our apps are model-agnostic, so you can switch vendors or self-host later. Simple tasks route to cheap models automatically.

How do you stop the AI from making costly mistakes?+

Engineering, not hope: constrained output schemas, approval gates before real outbound actions, retrieval instead of free generation for facts, an evaluation harness with golden cases, human breaks on high-stakes steps and full audit logs.

Is our data used to train models?+

Not on our watch. We use retrieval from your controlled storage, keep PII scoped, sign data-processing terms and refuse training on your business data by default. Your knowledge stays yours.

Will it feel fast to our customers?+

We build to a latency budget: around a second or less to the first token in chat with streaming output, and voice agents tuned with stricter real-call testing rather than scripted demos.

How do we know it's worth the money?+

We agree KPIs in the ROI assessment — minutes and hours saved, cost per completed task, error and escalation rates — baseline them before build, then review the before/after monthly against the investment.

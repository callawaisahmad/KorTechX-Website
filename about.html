Where we started

Built for the businesses agencies forget

KorTechX started in the field, not the boardroom. We kept taking over websites from agencies that had shipped slow, template-driven pages to tradespeople and local brands — owners paying for a page that would not make the phone ring. So we rebuilt those sites by hand, watched calls and bookings move, and realised the pattern: most small businesses are oversold and under-engineered.

Our team is small and senior on purpose. Every project is run by the engineers who will actually build it — no account-manager telephone game, no juniors learning on your invoice. We work remote-first so your project can find us across the UK, USA, Canada and Spain, and we measure success in the same units you do: jobs booked, orders placed, hours saved.

Engineering standards

The bar every build must clear

⚡ Performance budgets

Core Web Vitals gated in CI — LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1 — reviewed against real field data, not just lab scores.

🧬 Clean, owned code

Hand-written and documented. Semantic markup, no page-builder bloat, no template lock-in. Your next team should thank us, not curse us.

🔐 Security & data first

OWASP-aware review, least-privilege auth, secrets in vaults, encryption in transit and at rest. Privacy is engineered in, not bolted on.

🧰 Guardrailed AI

For automation work: retrieval over hallucination, approval gates on real actions, audit logs and an evaluation harness before anything touches customers.

♿ Accessible by default

WCAG 2.2 AA checked with automated tools plus a manual pass — keyboards, focus, contrast, screen readers.

🔭 Measured, not assumed

Consent-first analytics, event schemas, and reporting tied to revenue outcomes — the architecture for knowing what worked.

Our lines in the sand

What we won't do

  • No SEO spam. No bought link networks, no keyword-stuffed pages, no guaranteed-#1 promises. Rankings come from real technical health and content that earns attention.
  • No page-builder bloat. If a template is genuinely the right tool we'll say so, but we won't ship 800KB of bundled jQuery and tell you it's engineering.
  • No bill-by-the-hour surprises. Fixed scope means fixed price; scope changes are called out before they cost you money.
  • No data roulette. We don't train on your content by default, and we don't wire your customer data into AI without a plan for who can see it.
  • No handover hostage-taking. You own the code, the accounts and the DNS from day one. Exit-friendly by design.

Frequently asked questions

What makes KorTechX different from a regular web agency?+

We are engineers first. That means hand-written code with performance budgets enforced in CI, security reviewed as part of the build, and a complete handover so you own everything. You get the outcome (more jobs, orders, hours saved), not a flashy deck.

What size of business do you work with?+

From one-van tradespeople to funded startups and multi-branch service businesses, mainly in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the UAE. Engagement models run from fixed-scope projects to monthly retainers and technical advisory.

How quickly do projects typically start?+

Discovery usually begins within a week of an enquiry. Websites typically launch in 2–4 weeks from kickoff; MVPs are commonly scoped over 6–12 weeks; custom software and automation get a roadmap and milestones agreed up front.

Do you work remotely or on-site?+

Both, skewed remote-first. Our team spans multiple countries and time zones, and everything — code, docs, dashboards, calls — is collaborative and transparent. On-site workshops are possible for larger engagements.

Our business is new to tech. Will that be a problem?+

No. Most of our clients are not technical. We translate between your business goals and engineering reality, explain choices in plain language, and hand over systems your team can actually run.

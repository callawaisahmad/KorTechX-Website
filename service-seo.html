Under the hood

The technical detail

A precis of how we actually work — the standards, stack and checks behind seo.

Technical SEO

Healthy crawl and render beat chasing meta tags. We handle robots directives, canonical and parameter (facet) control, XML sitemaps, schema.org, and index-coverage monitoring in Search Console — including log-level crawl analysis for large sites.

  • Core Web Vitals treated as a ranking input: LCP/INP/CLS budgets, image and font pipelines, first-render audits
  • Custom 404 and redirect management with zero dead-end chains
  • Site architecture review so authority flows through topic clusters instead of orphan pages

Content, intent and entities

We map exact search intent, then build topic clusters of support and money pages that interlink. Copy answers the question first (which also earns AI Overview and generative-search citations), supported by JSON-LD facts.

  • Entity-rich content: precise service categories, geo-service-area pages, and visible expertise signals (E-E-A-T)
  • Structured Q&A blocks and FAQ schema where genuinely useful — not stuffing
  • Internal anchor strategy that tells both crawlers and readers what the next page is about

Local dominance and authority

For local businesses the map pack is the prize. We optimise Google Business Profile, build structured citations for NAP consistency, manage review velocity policy-safely, and strengthen relevance/district prominence signals.

  • LocalBusiness and service-area schema where applicable
  • White-hat links: digital PR, local press, relevant directories and unlinked-brand-mention recovery — no bought link networks
  • Honest timelines: technical wins in weeks, competitive rankings typically compounding over 3–6 months

FAQ

Common questions

How long until I see SEO results?+

Technical wins can show in weeks; competitive rankings typically build over 3–6 months as authority compounds.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?+

No credible agency can guarantee a specific position. We guarantee sound strategy, transparent reporting and durable, white-hat work.

What tools do you use and do we get access?+

Search Console, crawl and log analytics, rank and visibility trackers, GA4 and rich-results tooling. You get read-only access to everything plus a monthly report covering rankings, index coverage, Core Web Vitals and actions taken.

How long until 'near me' rankings move?+

Technical fixes show in weeks — crawl, render and coverage issues get resolved quickly. Competitive map-pack movement typically compounds over 3–6 months as citations, reviews and local prominence build. No credible partner guarantees a position or a date.

Do backlinks still matter for a local business?+

Yes, but local businesses earn more per unit of effort from Google Business Profile, citations and review velocity first. White-hat links — local press, community, genuinely relevant directories — matter once the fundamentals are solid.

How do we get cited in AI Overviews and generative search?+

By being the most citable answer: question-first copy, concise definitions, structured Q&A, facts published as schema, real expertise and outbound sources. We optimise for entities and answer precision, which is what generative engines reward.

How do you handle negative reviews and spam listings?+

Policy-compliant response workflows, reporting spam via Google Business Profile tools and review-generation practices that stay inside the rules. We never suggest buying reviews or review-gating schemes.
